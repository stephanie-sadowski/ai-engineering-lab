# Repairs & Tune-ups

## Partner shops
We have 240 partner repair shops in the US. Find the nearest one at lumenbikes.example/shops.

## Tune-up pricing
A standard tune-up costs $89 and includes brake and gear adjustment, a safety check, and chain lubrication. Lumen Plus members get one free tune-up per year.

## First-ride check
Every new bike includes a free first-ride check within 60 days of delivery, at any partner shop.

## E-bike service
E-bike motors and batteries must be serviced at a certified e-bike partner shop. Servicing elsewhere voids the battery warranty.
