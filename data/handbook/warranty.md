# Warranty

## Frame warranty
All Lumen frames carry a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects for the original owner. The warranty does not transfer when a bike is resold.

## Components
Components such as brakes, gears and wheels are covered for 2 years. Batteries on e-bikes are covered for 3 years or 800 charge cycles, whichever comes first.

## What is not covered
Normal wear (tires, brake pads, chains), crash damage, racing use, and damage from unauthorized modifications are not covered.

## Making a claim
Email warranty@lumenbikes.example with your order number, photos, and a description. Claims are reviewed within 3 business days. Approved claims are repaired or replaced at no cost, including shipping.
