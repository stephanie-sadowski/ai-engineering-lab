# Lumen Plus Membership

## Price
Lumen Plus costs $79 per year and renews automatically. You can cancel anytime; cancellations take effect at the end of the billing year and are not prorated.

## Benefits
Members get a 60-day return window, free express shipping, one free annual tune-up at any partner shop, and 15% off accessories.

## Crash replacement
Members who crash their bike can buy a replacement frame at 50% off, once per membership year.
