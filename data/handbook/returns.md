# Returns & Refunds

## Return window
Unused bikes and accessories can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Members of Lumen Plus get an extended 60-day return window.

## Condition requirements
Bikes must have fewer than 50 km on the odometer and no visible damage. Accessories must be in original packaging. Helmets cannot be returned once the hygiene seal is broken.

## Refund method and timing
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the warehouse inspects the return. Store credit is issued instantly and includes a 10% bonus.

## Return shipping
Return shipping is free for defective items. For change-of-mind returns, a $49 return shipping fee is deducted from bike refunds and $8 from accessory refunds.
