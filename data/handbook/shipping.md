# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 5-8 business days. Express shipping takes 2-3 business days and costs $39. Bikes ship in a single box, 85% pre-assembled.

## Free shipping
Orders over $150 ship free with standard shipping. Lumen Plus members get free express shipping on every order.

## International orders
We ship to Canada and the EU. International orders take 10-15 business days. Customs duties are paid by the customer and are not refundable.

## Lost or damaged packages
Report damaged packages within 48 hours of delivery with photos. Packages marked delivered but not received must be reported within 7 days; we will open a carrier claim and ship a replacement once the claim is approved.
